## full_body.glb (+ `../data/full_body_manifest.json`)

- Modifications by the Dynamic Human Anatomy Engine project: everything in upper_limbs.glb, plus the axial skeleton (skull, cervical/thoracic/lumbar spine, rib cage and sternum, pelvis) and both lower limbs (femur, patella, tibia, fibula, foot bones), a full-body armature (pelvis root, spine chain, hip/knee/ankle/toe joints) and 80 trunk / lower-limb muscle groups merged from Z-Anatomy parts. Of these 80 groups, 56 are static (skin weights transferred from the nearest bone surface, no shape keys) and 24 (12 groups, left and right) are dynamic: they additionally have `bulge` / `shorten` / `stretch` shape keys and estimated origin, insertion and via points (approximate). Foot contact points used for foot planting are derived at runtime from the foot bone meshes and are not stored in the asset. Meshes are decimated for size. Joint centers/axes are estimated from the mesh (approximate).
- Generated by `tools/blender/build_upper_limbs.py` + `tools/blender/body_ext.py` (`pnpm model:body`).
- This file and its manifest are distributed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.

## upper_limbs.glb (+ `../data/upper_limbs_manifest.json`)

- Modifications by the Dynamic Human Anatomy Engine project: extracted both upper limbs (clavicle, scapula, humerus, radius, ulna, hand skeleton and 15 muscle groups per side, including grip muscles), merged muscle heads into groups, added an armature with finger bones and skin weights, added `bulge` / `shorten` / `stretch` shape keys, and estimated joint centers/axes, attachment points and grip geometry from the mesh (approximate).
- Generated by `tools/blender/build_upper_limbs.py` (`pnpm model:upper`).
- This file and its manifest are distributed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.

## arm_zanatomy_l.glb (+ `../data/arm_zanatomy_manifest.json`)

- Modifications: extracted the left upper limb, merged muscle heads, added an armature with skin weights, and added contract/stretch shape keys (legacy M1 asset).
- This file and its manifest are distributed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.

Procedural placeholder geometry generated by `tools/blender/build_arm_sample.py`. Distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 for consistency with the other assets.
